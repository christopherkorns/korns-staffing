For job seekers

Work with Oklahoma employers. We’ll make the introduction.

If you can do the job and you can show up, we want to hear from you. Temporary assignments and direct-hire roles — depending on what local employers need right now.

Introduce yourself

What to send us

A résumé helps. So does a short note: the kind of work you want, where you can travel in Oklahoma, and when you can start. We staff industrial, professional, and administrative roles — not every job in the state, but a wide, practical set.

What happens next

  • We read what you sent. If we have a match, we call or write.
  • If we don’t have something yet, we keep you in mind. We will not invent openings to keep you on the line.
  • You interview with the employer. You decide if the job is yours to take.

No fees from you for a basic introduction

Staffing firms are typically paid by the employer. If anything about pay, assignment length, or paperwork is unclear, ask before you start. We’d rather you walk in with eyes open.

Be specific

Shift you can work. Tools you already know. Towns you can reach from Oklahoma City or elsewhere in the state. Specifics beat a generic “looking for anything.”
