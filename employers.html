For employers

Hire without the runaround.

Korns Staffing LLC works with Oklahoma employers who need people on the floor, in the office, or in a professional seat. We keep the brief short and the follow-up honest.

Start a hiring conversation

What you can ask us to staff

We place people in industrial, professional, and administrative work. That’s a working mix, not a claim that we own every trade in the state. If a role is outside what we can do well, we’ll say so.

Temp and direct hire

Some openings need a person next week. Some need someone who will still be there next year. We support both: temporary coverage and direct-hire searches. You tell us the arrangement; we recruit to it.

How we work with you

  • You describe the job in plain language — duties, schedule, pay range if you have one.
  • We screen for skills and fit before we send names.
  • You interview. You decide. We stay in the loop through start.

We do not list clients here. A conversation is a better proof than a logo wall.

Bring a real opening

The contact form is a demo on this site. Use it to draft what you’d send: role, location in Oklahoma, temp or hire. When live intake is ready, that information will already be in one place.
