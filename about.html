About

A local firm. A real person behind it.

Korns Staffing LLC is a locally owned staffing company based in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. Christopher Korns is the founder.

What we are

We exist to help Oklahoma employers hire and to help people find work — temporary and direct hire. The mix we handle is industrial, professional, and administrative staffing. We describe it that way because it is a fair picture of a general staffing practice, not because we are listing named industries or trophy accounts.

What we are not claiming

This site does not publish a street address, phone number, years in business, placement totals, or client logos. Those details are not on the public record we are using here, and we will not invent them. When the firm is ready to share a public phone or office, it will belong on the contact page — not in a footnote we made up.

Founder

Christopher Korns lives in the Oklahoma City area and started Korns Staffing LLC. If you write, you are writing to a small firm, not a national brand with a slogan committee.
